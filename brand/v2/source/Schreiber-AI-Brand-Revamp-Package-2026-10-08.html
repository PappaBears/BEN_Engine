01Executive summary

What we found.

Three senior reviewers went through every chapter of your kit: brand strategy, visual identity, and marketing. The voice is your strongest asset. The weakest areas are proof, production files and sales tools.

Each bar is a 0–10 score on the same scale. Lane totals are simple averages.

The five moves in this package

  1. Finish the logo as real drawings.

    Your mark was live type inside a mask, so every printer, sign shop and email client would draw it differently. We rebuilt it as outlined vector masters with one stroke weight, a dedicated small-size version, and a full file set.

  2. Give the brand a message, not just a motto.

    A promise, three message pillars, a descriptor that names what you sell, 10- and 30-second pitches, a boilerplate paragraph and a call-to-action library.

  3. Make proof a system.

    Every pillar has a proof slot and every touchpoint has a number in it. Slots stay empty, marked yellow, until a real client result fills them. Nothing is made up.

  4. Fix the jobs your colors do.

    Amber goes back to one job: the button. Status gets its own caution yellow and a cyan for info, so no status color matches a brand color.

  5. Build the selling kit.

    Eight touchpoints from first handshake to Google review, each with a trackable link back to one landing page.

03Identity craft

The same mark, finished.

You'll recognize it instantly. The difference is under the hood: outlined paths instead of live type, one stroke weight across plate, S and rivets, the Ai sitting on the S baseline with a clean 3.5-unit gap, and a separate drawing for small sizes.

Small sizes get their own drawing

At 16 px the old hollow S was half a pixel wide and turned to mush. Below 48 px the mark switches to a filled plate with a solid S. 32 px keeps the Ai; 16 px is the S alone.

Lockup proportions

Wordmark cap height is 0.38 of the mark, the gap is 0.30, and the caps are centred on the plate. Wordmark is Archivo ExtraBold at full width with +2% tracking, outlined, so no letters get squeezed to fit.

The mark says "Ai", the wordmark says "AI". That's deliberate: lowercase i only lives inside the mark.
