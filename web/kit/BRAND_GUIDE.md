# Schreiber AI × BEN Engine — brand guide (v1)

**Positioning.** Schreiber AI builds AI infrastructure for businesses that cannot and should not become data-center operators. BEN Engine is the hardware expression of that: a finished, measured, documented box.

**Voice.** Plain, specific, measured. We say what we verified and what we did not. We show risks before prices. We never say "cutting-edge", "seamless", "revolutionary". We say "20 ft high-cube, 96 kW IT, N+1, 225 A main, PUE 1.37 estimated".

## Names
- Company: **Schreiber AI** (two words, capital S, capital AI). Monogram: **SAI**.
- Product: **BEN Engine** (BEN capitalised, Engine title case). Expansion: *Business Edge Node*.
- Tiers: **BEN 5, BEN 30, BEN 150, BEN 500**. Modules: Power, Cooling, Compute, Controls.
- Software: **BEN Controls** (gateway + nodes + dashboard + twin).

Measured contrast on graphite: text 16.9:1, muted 9.6:1, cyan 13.1:1, amber 12.6:1 (all pass WCAG AA/AAA for normal text). On cyan buttons use graphite text (13.1:1).

## Type
Inter (display and body), JetBrains Mono for data/code. Scale in `brand.json`. Numbers always carry units. Tables over prose for data.

## Logo
`logo-sai.svg` (monogram) and `wordmark-ben-engine.svg`. Minimum size 24 px. Clear space = height of the amber dot × 2. Never on photographs, never recoloured except full white or full graphite.

## Imagery
Only generated illustrations from the configuration model (isometric heroes, drawings, renders) and real screenshots of the dashboard. No stock photos, no stock "AI" imagery, no borrowed logos. Every screenshot is of the real software; simulation mode is labelled.

## Watermarking
Any preview sent to a prospect carries "Schreiber AI — preview" in the footer. Editable sources stay with Schreiber AI until payment.
