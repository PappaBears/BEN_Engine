# BEN Engine brand guide, Rev 2

<img src="lockup-signal.svg" alt="Schreiber AI" height="40">

**BEN Engine, by Schreiber AI.** This guide applies the Schreiber AI brand package v2 (`brand/v2/source/Schreiber-AI-Brand-Revamp-Package-2026-10-08.html`, VIP Experience Team, 2026-10-08) to the BEN Engine product line, as decided in `docs/REV2_DECISION.md` §1. The Rev 1 guide is kept at `archive/rev1/web/kit/BRAND_GUIDE.md`.

## 1. Architecture and names

Clear space: 0.30 × mark on all sides. Minimum: mark 16 px screen / 0.25 in print; horizontal lockup 120 px / 1.25 in.

## 6. The data plate band (signature device)

A thin strip that reads like a machine nameplate. On hardware it is also the nameplate's public face.

* **Fields (hardware):** `Schreiber AI · BEN Engine · <tier> · S/N <serial> · Rated <kW> kW IT · <voltage> · <IP rating> · Rev 2`, e.g. `SCHREIBER AI │ BEN ENGINE │ BEN 300 │ S/N BEN300-2646-0001 │ RATED 300 kW IT │ 480 V 3φ │ IP66 │ REV 2`. Documents swap fields for document data (`Proposal · SA-… · Rated … · Page 2 / 6`, `Sales sheet · Rev 2 · 1 / 1`, slide `n / N`).
* **Geometry:** height 2.6 em; padding 1.4 em; gap 1.6 em; text IBM Plex Mono 600 caps, tracking +16 %; a rivet at each end (0.7 em circle, ground fill, 1.5 px Steel inner ring); 1 px × 1 em Border dividers; the lead word in Signal Blue, the rest in Fog. Two rows on items narrower than ~1.6 m.
* **Grounds:** screen and BEN 1 hardware: S2 `#0F2342` with Border rules. Outdoor hardware on Shop White: Night Navy `#06101F`. Print on white paper: Paper `#EEF4FC` ground, Slate text, Deep Cobalt lead.
* **QR plate** at the right end of every hardware band: square modules, error correction M, 4-module quiet zone, dark (Night Navy) on a Shop White plate, never reversed; code ≥ 0.8 in on small items, ≥ 2.5 in on module and cabinet doors; target `https://schreiberai.com/ben/<serial>?src=plate`.
* Where: module side and door, cabinet side and door, BEN 1 bezel and door, web footer, dashboard status strip, slides, proposals, drawings' title blocks, sales sheet. Production art: `brand/v2/livery/<tier>-band.svg`.

## 8. Print starting points

These are starting points to proof against a physical swatch book with the printer (package §04). Never approve a color from a screen.

| Color | Pantone start | Print build | Thread |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Signal Blue `#4C9DFF` | ≈ PMS 2727 C | proof | match to a physical Madeira or Isacord card |
| Deep Cobalt `#1E40AF` | ≈ PMS 2728 C | proof | match to card |
| Safety Amber `#FFB020` | ≈ PMS 1235 C | proof | match to card |
| Night Navy `#06101F` | ≈ PMS 2767 C or 5395 C | **rich black C60 M40 Y20 K100** (plain K100 prints as muddy black) | match to card |
| Shop White `#F4F7FB` | — | paint: match against the paint maker's fan deck; document: paper white | — |

* Starfield in print: stars ≥ 0.35 pt, coated stock only; on uncoated paper and newsprint use flat Night Navy.
* Module paint: Night Navy body is a powder or liquid coat matched to PMS 2767 C with a physical drawdown approved before production; the default outdoor body is Shop White with the Night Navy band.
* QR codes always print dark on light, never reversed, never on the starfield.

### Vinyl, signs and embroidery

* **Vinyl:** hand the sign shop the SVG; expand strokes in their software before cutting (the mark uses a stroke and a mask). Cast vinyl for corrugated container walls (heat-formed into the corrugations), printed laminated vinyl for the band and QR plate, minimum 5-year outdoor rating. Every type in the livery files is already outlined.
* **Embroidery:** minimum mark 0.75 in; satin S at 1.5 mm or wider; rivets as small fill dots; below 0.75 in use the small-mark drawing. Thread to the physical card.
* **Engraving / laser:** one-color masters (`*-white.svg`, `*-black.svg`), minimum engraved cap height 1.5 mm.
