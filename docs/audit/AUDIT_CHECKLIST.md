# BEN Engine — Internal Audit Checklist (reusable)

Companion to `docs/audit/AUDIT_REPORT_2026-11-08.md`. Use it before every supplier RFQ wave, investor review or engineering release. The mechanical items are automated in `scripts/audit.py` (static) and `scripts/audit.py --run` (also executes the build contract); the rest are inspections a person does with the commands shown.

Conventions: **Criterion** is the requirement audited against; **How** is the exact check; **Evidence** is what goes in the audit file; **Pass rule** is binary. Classify a miss as Major (a claim is false, a safety/contract item is inconsistent, or a buyer would be misled), Minor (a documented requirement is not met but the claim is honest), Observation (a weakness that is not yet a nonconformity) or OFI (opportunity for improvement). Record the commit (`git rev-parse --short HEAD`) and the date at the top of every audit file.

## Closing the audit

1. Save `scripts/audit.py` output (static and `--run`) as the appendix.
2. Count findings by classification; write the conclusion as release-ready yes / yes with conditions / no.
3. Enter every Major and Minor in the CAPA list with owner role and due date; re-audit the CAPA items before release.
