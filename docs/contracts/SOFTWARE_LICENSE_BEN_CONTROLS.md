## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**BEN Controls**" means Schreiber AI's controls layer for a Unit, in object code form unless this Licence says otherwise: (a) **Pod Firmware**: the firmware for BEN Pods (Silicon Labs MGM240P / EFR32MG24 with Secure Vault), including the Gecko bootloader configuration, the OpenThread stack configuration and the HAL; (b) **Edge Software**: the BEN Controller software: the edge computer operating system image (OnLogic Karbon 400 or, on BEN Controls Lite, the Revolution Pi RevPi Connect 4), the PLCnext project for the Phoenix Contact AXC F 2152 controller (interlock logic, watchdog, I/O mapping) and the `ben_engine` server (MQTT broker configuration, Modbus/SNMP pollers, alarm engine, digital twin, CFD solver, sweeps, commissioning, calibration and analysis tools, export API, backup jobs, firewall rules); (c) **Dashboard**: the web dashboard served by the gateway (live data, 3-D twin, walkthrough, sweeps, CSV export, I/O test, calibration screens, checklists); (d) **Documentation**: `spec/PROTOCOL.md`, `spec/INTERFACES.md`, the O&M manual, the SBOM and release notes; (e) **Updates**: patches, security roll-ups, new releases and new signed images delivered under this Licence.

1.2 "**Signed Image**" means a Pod Firmware or Edge Software image signed with Schreiber AI's Ed25519 release key (or, under Section 9.6, Licensee's own key) and verified by the device before installation.

1.3 "**Unit**" means the BEN Engine unit identified by [UNIT_SERIAL] in the SOW, including its BEN Controller (PLC and edge computer, or RevPi), BEN Pods and any replacement controller component or Pod installed under warranty, RMA or the spares kit.

1.4 "**Brand File**" means the configuration file in the format of `web/kit/brand.json` that sets the names, colours, logo and wordmark shown by the Dashboard.

1.5 "**Open Components**" means third-party software included in BEN Controls under open-source licences, listed in the SBOM delivered with each release.

1.6 "**Telemetry**" means the data generated by the Unit's Pods, PLC, edge computer and polled equipment, as described in `spec/PROTOCOL.md`.

1.7 "**Escrow Materials**" means the source code, build scripts, signing-key provisioning procedure (not the key itself), toolchain versions and documentation sufficient for a competent engineer to build, sign with a new key, and maintain BEN Controls for the Licensee's Units.

Other capitalised terms have the meanings in the MSA.

## 2. Licence grants by type

### 2.1 Type A — Perpetual per-Unit licence (hardware plus BEN Controls purchases)

Subject to payment of the licence fee [LICENSE_FEE] for the Unit, Schreiber AI grants Licensee a perpetual, non-exclusive, non-transferable (except under Section 11) licence to: (a) install and run the Pod Firmware on the Unit's Pods and spare Pods, and the Edge Software and Dashboard on the Unit's BEN Controller (PLC and edge computer) and one cold-spare edge computer; (b) use the Dashboard from any number of operator seats on Licensee's network; (c) use the Documentation to operate, maintain, integrate, permit and insure the Unit; (d) make backup copies of the Edge Software and database; (e) receive Updates for [UPDATE_ENTITLEMENT_MONTHS] months from SAT Acceptance (the "**Update Period**"), extendable by annual support fee [ANNUAL_SUPPORT_FEE]. After the Update Period, the licence to the then-installed version continues perpetually; security Updates under Section 8.4 remain available for [SECURITY_UPDATE_YEARS] years from the release date of the installed major version.

### 2.2 Type B — Subscription licence (Managed Services)

Subject to payment of the Managed Services fees under `docs/contracts/MANAGED_SERVICES_SLA.md`, Schreiber AI grants Licensee a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use BEN Controls on the Unit for the Managed Services term. Updates are included and applied by Schreiber AI during maintenance windows. If Managed Services end other than for Licensee's breach, the licence converts automatically to a Type A licence for the then-installed version at no further licence fee, with the Update Period deemed expired; Licensee may buy the annual support fee to resume Updates.

### 2.3 Type C — White-Label licence (Integrators and white-label Customers)

Subject to the Integrator Agreement or the SOW with the white-label flag set, and payment of the per-Unit white-label licence fee [WHITE_LABEL_LICENSE_FEE] (or the annual controls licence per tier stated in the Integrator Agreement), Schreiber AI grants Licensee a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to: (a) everything in Type A for each licensed Unit; (b) apply Licensee's Brand File to the Dashboard and the product labels as permitted by Section 5; (c) sublicense the Dashboard and Documentation to the end customer of each Unit under terms at least as protective of Schreiber AI as this Licence, naming Schreiber AI as a third-party beneficiary; (d) where the Integrator Agreement grants it, operate its own remote support endpoint (Z4) for its Units. Pod Firmware and Edge Software are never sublicensed in source form under Type C unless Section 9.6 applies.

### 2.4 Hardware-only purchases

A Unit bought without the Controls module carries no licence to BEN Controls except the minimum firmware needed for life-safety monitoring contacts, if any, which is licensed under Type A terms at no fee. Section 10 (escrow) is available to hardware-only Customers who later add the Controls module.

## 3. Restrictions

Licensee will not, and will not permit anyone to: (a) copy BEN Controls except as Section 2 allows; (b) run the Edge Software or Dashboard on hardware other than the Unit's BEN Controller and its spare, or run Pod Firmware on hardware other than BEN Pods supplied or approved by Schreiber AI; (c) **reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, extract keys from, or attempt to defeat the signature verification, secure boot, Secure Vault key storage, attestation or TPM-sealed storage of a Signed Image or device**, except to the extent applicable law expressly permits despite this restriction and then only after asking Schreiber AI for the interoperability information first; (d) modify BEN Controls, or install an unsigned or third-party-signed image on a Pod, the PLC or the edge computer, except as Section 9.6 allows; (e) remove, alter or obscure any safety notice, warning, manufacturer identification, licence notice or Open Components attribution (Section 5.3, Section 6); (f) use BEN Controls to operate a unit that is not a BEN Engine Unit built under a Schreiber AI SOW, Integrator Agreement or Contract Manufacturing Agreement; (g) disclose benchmark or twin-accuracy results that identify Schreiber AI without Schreiber AI's consent, except to the Licensee's own customers and insurers for the Unit; (h) use BEN Controls to control life-safety functions: fire suppression, EPO and HVAC shutdown on fire detection are hardwired by design (`spec/PROTOCOL.md` safety rules) and BEN Controls only monitors them; (i) use BEN Controls in violation of export control or sanctions law.

## 4. Licensee data and telemetry

4.1 **Ownership.** All Telemetry, alarms, calibration records, Commissioning Reports, video and access logs generated by the Unit belong to the Customer that owns or operates the Unit (MSA Section 15.1). Under Type C, they belong to the Integrator's end customer, not the Integrator, unless the Integrator's contract with the end customer says otherwise.

4.2 **Local-first.** BEN Controls stores Telemetry on the edge computer and operates with no internet connection. Schreiber AI will not release an Update that makes any function of BEN Controls needed to operate the Unit depend on a Schreiber AI or third-party cloud service, licence server or periodic online activation.

4.3 **Telemetry sharing opt-in.** Licensee may elect in the order form (Section 14) to share Telemetry with Schreiber AI through the outbound remote support path for: (a) operation and support of the Unit (required for Type B); (b) anonymised fleet statistics used to improve the digital twin, alarm thresholds and sizing rules. Anonymisation removes Unit serial, site name, coordinates, Customer name, Brand File, IP addresses and video before aggregation; Schreiber AI publishes only aggregates of at least [FLEET_MIN_UNITS] Units. Licensee may withdraw the opt-in at any time by notice or by disabling the remote support path with the key switch; withdrawal stops future sharing and does not affect aggregates already computed. Without the opt-in, Schreiber AI receives no Telemetry (Type A and C) except what Licensee sends with a support request.

4.4 **Support diagnostics.** Diagnostic bundles Licensee exports and sends for support are used only to resolve the support case and are deleted within 90 days of case closure unless Licensee asks otherwise.

## 5. White-label terms (Type C, and SOWs with the white-label flag)

5.1 **Brand substitution rights.** Licensee may replace the Dashboard theme and product names with those in its Brand File: product name, tier names, colours, display and mono fonts, logo and wordmark, tagline, support contact. The Brand File is applied by configuration, not by modifying code. Schreiber AI will provide a Brand File validator with each release.

5.2 **What may not be changed.** Licensee will not remove, hide or alter: (a) safety notices, warnings, alarm texts and alarm severities in the Dashboard; (b) the ASHRAE class limits, failsafe behaviour descriptions and entry procedure shown in the Dashboard; (c) the manufacturer identification in security logs, certificates, SBOM, firmware hashes and the device labels inside the controls cabinet, which remain "Schreiber AI" for traceability (`docs/CYBERSECURITY.md` Section 7); (d) the Open Components attribution screen (Section 6); (e) the Commissioning Report's statement of the script version and data hash.

5.3 **About screen attribution.** The Dashboard's About screen on every white-label Unit will display, in the Licensee's theme but in no smaller type than the Licensee's product name on that screen: "Powered by BEN Controls, a Schreiber AI technology. Firmware [FIRMWARE_VERSION] · Edge computer image [GATEWAY_IMAGE_VERSION] · Signing key [SIGNING_CERT_FINGERPRINT]" together with the Open Components attribution link. Licensee may add its own version and support information.

5.4 **Trademark use.** Schreiber AI grants Licensee a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence for the Licence term to use the marks "BEN Controls", "BEN Engine" and the Schreiber AI monogram solely in the About screen attribution, in the Documentation delivered to end customers, and in marketing approved under the Integrator Agreement. Licensee will follow `web/kit/brand.json` rules for the Schreiber AI marks (monogram in cyan on graphite or graphite on light; never on photographs; product name written "BEN Engine"; tier names "BEN 5", "BEN 30", "BEN 150", "BEN 500"). Licensee's own marks remain Licensee's; Schreiber AI acquires no rights in them except the right to apply them under this Section.

5.5 **Update obligations.** Licensee will: (a) apply or allow Schreiber AI to apply security Updates classified "critical" within [CRITICAL_UPDATE_DAYS] days of release and other security Updates within [SECURITY_UPDATE_DAYS] days; (b) keep every Unit it supports on a release that is within [SUPPORTED_RELEASE_WINDOW] major versions of the current release; (c) not withhold an Update from an end customer for commercial reasons; (d) pass vulnerability notices from Schreiber AI to end customers within 72 hours (`docs/CYBERSECURITY.md` Section 5 and 7); (e) tell Schreiber AI within 5 Business Days of learning of a vulnerability or incident affecting BEN Controls.

5.6 **Fleet separation.** An edge computer will manage Units signed with one key only. Units running Licensee-signed images under Section 9.6 and Units running Schreiber AI-signed images are never mixed on one edge computer or one remote support endpoint.

5.7 **End-customer statement.** Licensee's contract with each end customer will state that the controls layer is licensed from Schreiber AI, that end-customer data belongs to the end customer, that the unit operates with no cloud dependency, and will pass through Sections 3, 4, 6 and 8.5.

## 6. Open Components

6.1 BEN Controls includes Open Components (for example the edge computer operating system, MQTT broker, OpenThread Border Router, Python libraries, web libraries and the Silicon Labs Gecko SDK / OpenThread). Each is listed in the SBOM (SPDX) delivered with every release and shown in the Dashboard's Open Components attribution screen with its licence text.

6.2 Open Components are licensed to Licensee under their own licences, which prevail over this Licence for those components. Where an Open Component licence requires it, Schreiber AI will provide the corresponding source for that component on request.

6.3 Nothing in this Licence restricts rights granted by an Open Component's licence, and nothing in an Open Component's licence grants rights in Schreiber AI's own code.

## 7. Delivery, installation and keys

7.1 BEN Controls is delivered installed on the Unit at FAT, with the Pod firmware, PLC project and edge image hashes recorded on the FAT Certificate and in the Turnover Package (item G1). Updates are delivered as Signed Images through the remote support path (Type B, or Type A/C with opt-in) or as downloadable Signed Images with published SHA-256 hashes.

7.2 Schreiber AI holds the firmware signing key. Device certificates for Pods (generated inside the Pod's secure element at the provisioning cell; the private key never leaves the chip) and for the edge computer (TPM-bound) are issued by Schreiber AI's PKI at the factory, valid 5 years, with expiry shown in the Dashboard and a re-issue procedure in the O&M manual.

7.3 Licensee is responsible for the physical security of the controls cabinet and for the credentials handed over at Turnover.

## 8. Updates, support and vulnerability handling

8.1 **Release cadence.** Security roll-ups monthly or sooner for critical CVEs; feature releases as published. Updates are staged (digital twin, then Schreiber AI lab unit, then customer units in waves) with A/B images and automatic rollback if the device does not report healthy within 5 minutes (`docs/CYBERSECURITY.md` Section 5).

8.2 **Compatibility.** Updates will not remove a documented API in `spec/INTERFACES.md` or a documented MQTT topic or channel in `spec/PROTOCOL.md` without at least [API_DEPRECATION_MONTHS] months' notice and a documented migration.

8.3 **Support levels.** Type A: email support, response within 2 Business Days during the Update Period. Type B: per the Managed Services SLA. Type C: Integrator provides L1 support to end customers; Schreiber AI provides L2/L3 to the Integrator per the Integrator Agreement.

8.4 **Security Updates after the Update Period.** Schreiber AI will make security Updates for the installed major version available for the period in Section 2.1 at no charge, so that the Unit can be kept safe without buying feature Updates.

8.5 **Vulnerability disclosure.** Schreiber AI operates coordinated disclosure at security@schreiberai.com with a 90-day disclosure window and notifies affected Licensees within 72 hours of confirming an exploitable issue. Licensee may report vulnerabilities and will not publicly disclose one before the coordinated date.

## 9. Signed images and Licensee-controlled signing

9.1 Pods, the PLC and the edge computer verify the signature of every image before installation and reject and log unsigned or wrongly signed images; Pods additionally attest their boot state at every network join. This is tested at FAT and SAT (E12).

9.2 Licensee will not attempt to install an image that is not a Signed Image.

9.3 Schreiber AI will not use its signing key or the remote support path to install an Update on a Licensee Unit without Licensee's consent given in the SLA maintenance-window schedule (Type B) or per Update (Type A/C).

9.4 Schreiber AI will not deliver an Update whose purpose is to disable a Unit for non-payment. Remedies for non-payment are those in the MSA or Integrator Agreement.

9.5 Schreiber AI will keep the signing key in a hardware security module or equivalent, with access limited to named release engineers and logged.

9.6 **Full source licence option (Type C only).** If the Integrator Agreement grants the full source licence for fee [FULL_SOURCE_LICENSE_FEE], Licensee receives the Escrow Materials directly, may operate its own signing infrastructure, and its key is provisioned at the factory for its Units. Schreiber AI then cannot push firmware to those Units, Licensee assumes the update and vulnerability obligations in Section 5.5 and `docs/CYBERSECURITY.md` Section 5 for them, and Schreiber AI's warranty for BEN Controls on those Units is limited to the source as delivered.

## 10. Source code escrow option

10.1 **Election.** Licensee may elect escrow in the order form (Section 14) for an annual fee of [ESCROW_FEE_ANNUAL]. Escrow is available to Type A, Type C and hardware-only-then-controls Customers. Type B Licensees receive escrow at no fee for the Managed Services term.

10.2 **Deposit.** Schreiber AI will deposit the Escrow Materials with [ESCROW_AGENT] within 60 days of the election and will update the deposit within 30 days of each major release and at least every 6 months, with a verification level of [ESCROW_VERIFICATION_LEVEL].

10.3 **Release triggers.** The agent releases the Escrow Materials to Licensee if: (a) Schreiber AI becomes insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, or has a bankruptcy petition filed against it that is not dismissed within 60 days; (b) Schreiber AI ceases business or ceases to support BEN Controls generally without a successor assuming this Licence; (c) Schreiber AI fails to provide a security Update for a critical vulnerability within [ESCROW_SECURITY_TRIGGER_DAYS] days of its confirmation during a period when it is obliged to; (d) Schreiber AI materially breaches Section 4.2 (no cloud dependency) and does not cure within 60 days of notice.

10.4 **Licence on release.** On release Licensee receives a perpetual, non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use, modify and compile the Escrow Materials, and to sign with its own key, solely to maintain, secure and operate its own Units (and, for Type C, its end customers' Units). Licensee may engage contractors under confidentiality to do so. Licensee may not distribute the Escrow Materials or use them to build units not already licensed.

10.5 **Purpose.** The Parties record that escrow exists so that the "no cloud dependency" promise in `docs/CYBERSECURITY.md` Section 7 survives Schreiber AI (Risk R12).

## 11. Transfer with the Unit

A Type A or converted Type B licence transfers with the Unit to a buyer who agrees in writing to this Licence, provided Licensee deletes its copies other than those on the Unit and its spare, and provided the Unit is relocated under the Warranty Terms or after warranty expiry. Schreiber AI will re-issue device certificates to the new owner for the re-issue fee [CERT_REISSUE_FEE]. Type C licences transfer only with Schreiber AI's consent under the Integrator Agreement.

## 13. Term and termination

13.1 Type A licences are perpetual subject to Section 13.2. Type B licences last for the Managed Services term and convert under Section 2.2. Type C licences last for the Integrator Agreement term and survive for Units already deployed, so that end customers keep their Units running.

13.2 Schreiber AI may terminate this Licence for a Unit if Licensee materially breaches Section 3 or Section 5 and does not cure within 30 days of notice. Termination does not require or permit Schreiber AI to disable the Unit (Section 9.4); it ends Licensee's right to Updates and support and Licensee's rights under Section 5. Licensee's end customers' rights to operate deployed Units survive under the sublicence.

13.3 Sections 3, 4, 6, 9.4, 10.4, 11, 12 and 13 survive.
