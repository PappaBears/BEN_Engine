## 2. Confidential Information

2.1 "**Confidential Information**" means non-public information disclosed by the Discloser to the Recipient in any form on or after the Effective Date in connection with the Purpose that is marked or identified as confidential, or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential from its nature or the circumstances.

2.2 For Schreiber AI, Confidential Information includes: the configuration model and sizing rules; issue-for-construction drawings and CTQ/CTP tables; bills of materials with prices, supplier names and confirmation status; the parts catalog; STE assembly instructions and QC forms; firmware, edge computer software, dashboard and digital twin source or binaries not publicly released; commissioning, calibration and analysis methods and data; the digital twin's fitted parameters and validation results; unit economics, margins, pricing and discount schedules; the investor proposition; supplier and integrator terms; and the identity of customers and sites.

2.3 For Counterparty, Confidential Information includes: site information, utility data, network diagrams, security practices, IT architecture, business plans, pricing it discloses, and, for integrators and manufacturers, facility capabilities, certifications and customer lists.

2.4 **Exclusions.** Information is not Confidential Information if the Recipient can show it: (a) is or becomes publicly available without the Recipient's breach; (b) was known to the Recipient without restriction before disclosure; (c) is independently developed by the Recipient without use of or reference to the Discloser's Confidential Information; or (d) is rightfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality.

2.5 **Public materials.** Information Schreiber AI publishes on its product page, in its marketing kit or in the public repository is not Confidential Information. Samples, previews and proposals sent to prospects are watermarked "Schreiber AI" and are Confidential Information unless Schreiber AI says otherwise.

## 3. Obligations

3.1 The Recipient will: (a) use Confidential Information only for the Purpose; (b) not disclose it to anyone except its and its affiliates' employees, officers, professional advisers, and, with the Discloser's prior written consent, contractors, who need to know it for the Purpose and are bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this NDA; (c) protect it with at least the care it uses for its own confidential information of similar importance and not less than reasonable care; (d) not reverse engineer, decompile or analyse the composition of any sample, firmware image, BEN Pod or software provided under this NDA; (e) not remove confidentiality or watermark notices; (f) promptly notify the Discloser of any unauthorised use or disclosure and cooperate to limit the harm.

3.2 **Compelled disclosure.** The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation, court order or stock exchange rule, provided it gives the Discloser prompt notice where lawful, cooperates with efforts to obtain protective treatment, and discloses only what is required.

3.3 **No obligation to proceed.** Nothing in this NDA obliges either Party to disclose information, to enter into any further agreement, or to refrain from doing business with others. Each Party remains free to develop products and services competitive with the other's, provided it does not use the other's Confidential Information to do so.

3.4 **Site visits and factory visits.** A Party visiting the other's site or factory will follow its safety and security rules, will not photograph or record without consent, and treats what it observes as Confidential Information of the host.

## 4. Ownership and no licence

4.1 Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser. No licence or other right is granted except the limited right to use it for the Purpose. Feedback a Party volunteers about the other's products may be used by the other without obligation, but this does not transfer any Confidential Information.

4.2 No warranty is given about the accuracy or completeness of Confidential Information; it is provided "as is" for evaluation. Prices, lead times and performance figures in Schreiber AI proposals are subject to a signed Statement of Work.

## 5. Term and return

5.1 This NDA governs disclosures made during 3 years from the Effective Date, unless earlier terminated by either Party on 30 days' written notice.

5.2 The Recipient's obligations continue for 5 years after the date of each disclosure, and for trade secrets (including firmware and software source, the configuration model and fitted twin parameters, and bills of materials with prices) for as long as they remain trade secrets under applicable law.

5.3 On the Discloser's written request, or when the Purpose ends, the Recipient will return or destroy all Confidential Information and certify destruction in writing within 30 days, except that it may keep one archival copy in its legal files and copies in routine backups that are not accessed, subject to this NDA.
