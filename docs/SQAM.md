This manual is written in plain language. Where it says **shall**, the requirement is contractual. Where it says **should**, the requirement is expected and a deviation needs Schreiber AI agreement in writing.

## 1. Purpose, scope and applicability

**Purpose.** BEN Engine units are built by third parties from Schreiber AI's drawings, parts list and Simplified Technical English instructions. This manual tells those parties what quality system they need, which characteristics are critical, how each one is measured and recorded, where work stops for a Schreiber AI signature, and what happens when something is out of tolerance.

**Scope.** Everything between receipt of the enclosure and parts and hand-over of the unit on the transport vehicle, plus site set-down where the same party does it. It covers the 5 tiers (BEN 5, BEN 30, BEN 150, BEN 500 and BEN 2000) and every subsystem (structure, envelope, power, cooling, compute mounting, controls, fire and life safety).

| Party | Applicability |
|---|---|
| Contract manufacturer (CM) | All sections. The CM is responsible for the hold and witness points inside its shop and for the sub-tier suppliers it uses (welders, electricians, HVAC, fire installer). |
| System integrator | Sections 1-4, 5 (controls and electrical items), 6 (electrical, controls, final/FAT), 8-16. |
| Component supplier (racks, UPS, cooling units, PLC and I/O, edge computer, BEN Pods, UL 508A panel shop, cabinets) | Sections 1, 2 (quality system), 4, 9, 10 (serial numbers and certificates), 12, 15. The supplier's own CTQs are the catalogue specification referenced on the purchase order. |

**Definitions.**

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| CTQ — critical to quality | A measurable characteristic of the finished unit that, if out of tolerance, affects safety, listing, performance or the customer's acceptance. Every CTQ has a specification, a method, an instrument and a record. |
| CTP — critical to process | A process step that cannot be verified by inspecting the result alone (welding, torque, brazing, evacuation, licensed electrical work). CTPs are controlled by qualified people, calibrated tools and a witnessed or held record. |
| Hold point | Work stops. It restarts only after the named qualified person (and Schreiber AI quality where stated) signs the form. A hold point is never waived by the CM. |
| Witness point | The QC inspector (Schreiber AI or its delegated inspector) is present and signs. If the inspector was notified 48 h ahead and did not attend, the CM may proceed and records it. |
| NCR — non-conformance report | The record of anything that does not meet a drawing, this manual or the instructions, with its disposition. |
| CAPA | Corrective and preventive action: the fix for the cause of an NCR, with evidence that it worked. |
| FAI — first-article inspection | The complete dimensional and functional inspection of the first unit of each tier built by a supplier, witnessed 100 % by Schreiber AI. |
| FAT | Factory acceptance test: the pre-ship test run with the Schreiber AI commissioning script, producing a signed report and data hash. |
| Deviation request | The form that asks Schreiber AI Engineering to accept a departure from a drawing or specification before the work is done. |

## 3. Roles and the Schreiber AI quality presence

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Schreiber AI Engineering (design authority) | Owns the configuration model, drawings, CTQ/CTP register and instructions. Approves deviations, use-as-is dispositions and design changes. Nobody else changes a drawing. |
| Schreiber AI Quality | Approves suppliers (audit), attends hold and witness points, reviews Q-forms, runs or witnesses the FAT, signs the certificate of conformance, keeps the scorecard. |
| Schreiber AI Field / Commissioning | Runs the commissioning script at FAT and at site; signs the hand-over. |
| Supplier quality lead (named person) | Single point of contact. Keeps the unit's QC file, raises NCRs, calls hold/witness points 48 h ahead, closes CAPAs. |
| Supplier qualified persons | Certified welder, licensed electrician, EPA 608 technician, fire installer, CSC surveyor, rigger: each signs the hold point in their trade. |
| Workers | Follow the STE instructions step by step, record values on the Q-forms, stop at every hold point. |

Presence rules: first article of each tier — Schreiber AI Quality present for 100 % of hold and witness points and the full FAT. Units 2-3 — witness points and FAT. Standard surveillance after that — FAT always, hold points by sampling (at least one per unit), all Q-forms reviewed before shipment. Surveillance increases to first-article level after any critical NCR.

## 4. Document control

- Build only from a drawing set whose title block reads **ISSUE FOR CONSTRUCTION** with a named checker (current set: drawing set Rev B (2026-11-08, ISSUED FOR REVIEW); `design/REVISION.json`) for the unit's configuration: `design/drawings/<tier>/A-001 … C-001`, revision letter in the title block, together with `design/assembly/ASSEMBLY-<tier>.md`, `QC_FORMS.md` and this manual. The configuration line in the title block (tier, kW, racks, cooling, redundancy, climate) must match the purchase order.
- Drawing numbers: A- architecture, S- structure, E- electrical, M- mechanical/cooling, C- controls; sheets are numbered n/7. Superseded revisions are stamped SUPERSEDED and removed from the bay.
- Red-lines: the supplier marks as-built changes in red on a copy of the IFC sheet, dated and initialled, within 24 h of the change. Red-lines are scanned into the unit's QC file and sent to Schreiber AI Engineering, which regenerates the drawing set (the drawings are generated from the configuration model, so a red-line becomes a model or generator change, not a hand edit).
- Deviation request (form DR-01): any departure from a drawing, this manual or the instructions is requested **before** the work, with the reason, the proposed alternative and the CTQs affected. Schreiber AI Engineering answers in writing within 2 working days. No verbal approvals.
- Records are kept for the life of the unit plus 10 years; the unit QC file is handed over with the turnover package.

## 5. CTQ / CTP register

The register is generated per tier from `design/ctq.py`. The same items appear on the drawing sheet named in the characteristic column and as "Check:" lines in the assembly task that produces the feature. Instrument accuracy required is one quarter of the tolerance (or 5 % of a one-sided limit); the instrument column gives the typical instrument and its accuracy so the supplier can confirm its own equipment qualifies.

## 7. First-article inspection (FAI)

- The first unit of each tier built by a supplier (and the first unit after a move of the production bay, a change of key personnel, or 6 months without a build) is a first article.
- Scope: 100 % of the CTQ/CTP register with every value recorded (no sampling); full dimensional check of the equipment schedule on A-002 (every tag x, y, z position within +/-10 mm); full functional test per section 8 at 50 % and 100 % load; label walk-down of every tag on A-002, C-001 and E-001; photograph record of every hold point.
- Schreiber AI Quality witnesses 100 % of hold and witness points and the FAT. Schreiber AI Engineering reviews the FAI report before the unit ships.
- Record: form FAI-01 (balloon numbers keyed to the drawing dims, measured value, instrument ID, pass/fail) plus the complete Q-01…Q-12 set, the NCR log and the FAT report. The FAI report is approved in writing before the second unit of that tier starts.

## 8. Factory acceptance test (FAT) linkage

- The FAT is the pre-ship test of Task 11 run with the Schreiber AI commissioning script. In the factory the script runs in simulation-assisted mode against the real BEN Controller (PLC or RevPi, edge computer) and BEN Pods (`python -m ben_engine.commissioning --live --url <gateway> --tier <tier> --out FAT-<serial>.md --operator <name>`); where the controller is not yet networked, the `--sim` run proves the controls software path and is attached as a separate report, never as the FAT.
- Load: resistive load bank on the rack PDUs at 50 % for 1 h and 100 % for 2 h; utility-loss test on battery; EPO test (< 1 s, witnessed).
- Output: the markdown report, the telemetry CSV and the CSV's SHA-256 hash printed in the report. The report is valid only with a data file whose hash matches. Schreiber AI Quality signs the certificate of conformance (form COC-01) referencing the report hash, the unit serial number and the IFC revision.
- Acceptance: all CTQ-M5 / CTQ-E3 limits met, no open critical or major NCR, all Q-forms complete, FAI approved (first unit).

## 9. Nonconformance, disposition and corrective action

- Anything that does not meet a drawing, the register, the instructions or this manual is recorded on an NCR (form NCR-01) within one shift of discovery. The feature is tagged. Work on that feature stops.
- Classification: **critical** — safety, listing or a CTQ out of tolerance; **major** — function or durability; **minor** — cosmetic or documentation.
- Disposition is one of: **rework** to the drawing and re-inspect 100 %; **use-as-is** only with Schreiber AI Engineering approval written on the NCR (never for critical); **scrap / replace**. Anything touching a CTQ is Schreiber AI's decision.
- CAPA (form CAPA-01, 8D-lite): D1 team, D2 problem, D3 containment (which other units / features are affected), D4 root cause (5-why), D5 corrective action, D6 verification evidence, D7 prevention (procedure / instruction / fixture change), D8 close-out. Required for every critical NCR, for any major NCR repeated on two units, and when a hold point is found bypassed.
- Escape escalation: a nonconformance found after shipment (site, commissioning, warranty) is a **quality escape**. The supplier is informed within 24 h, a CAPA is mandatory, surveillance returns to first-article level for the next unit, and the escape counts in the scorecard (section 14).

## 11. Workmanship standards

**Torque (ISO 898-1 class 8.8, dry, calibrated wrench, +/-10 %, paint stripe after witness):**

| Size | N·m |
|---|---|
| M8 | 25 |
| M10 | 50 |
| M12 | 85 |
| M16 | 210 |
| M20 | 410 |

Electrical lugs and breaker terminals: the value on the device label, recorded per lug (CTP-E2). Never re-use a nyloc nut. Never torque over insulation or shims that can creep.

- **Labelling:** every item carries the tag from A-002 / C-001 / E-001 (R1, CU2, UPS1, PNL-M, N1, T_in_r1_0.4 …) on a laminated or engraved label, 10 mm text minimum, at the top front; every cable at both ends; every breaker, receptacle and terminal. Sensor names are the PROTOCOL.md channel names exactly. Arc-flash and warning labels per E-001 notes.
- **Cable management:** power in tray TR-P, data in TR-D, 300 mm apart, crossings at 90 degrees; bend radius >= 8 x cable diameter (4 x for Cat6A); hook-and-loop ties only on data; no cable rests on a sharp edge; service loops 300 mm at Pods and at the panel; separate life-safety wiring (FRP, EPO) in its own conduit, red-identified.
- **Conduit and glands:** liquid-tight or EMT inside; every shell penetration through a welded frame or curb with an EPDM boot or IP66 gland; fire-stop where required; bonding bushings on metallic conduit.
- **Penetration sealing:** butyl + EPDM boot on roof curbs, sleeved and sealed pipe penetrations, PU sealant on every liner lap; smoke-pencil test (CTQ-C2).
- **Welding:** AWS D1.1 visual acceptance; no undercut > 0.5 mm, no porosity, full fillet both sides of reinforcement frames; grind flush where a seal lands.
- **Paint and corrosion:** cut edges and welds primed within 4 h with zinc-rich primer; topcoat to the corrosion class on A-001 (C4-M min, C5-M coastal); touch-up any chip > 2 mm before shipment; dissimilar metals isolated.
- **Housekeeping:** no swarf in the envelope before the liner closes (magnet sweep); no debris in trays; filters fitted before the first fan run.

## 12. Packaging, transport preparation and handling

- Follow Task 12 of the instructions and record on Q-11. Containers: CSC re-inspection after modification (HOLD, CTP-A2); 4-leg lift on top castings only, sling angle >= 60 degrees (HOLD, CTP-S4); tie-down by the bottom castings; doors sealed and seal number recorded.
- Batteries at 30-50 % SoC, disconnect open, dangerous-goods paperwork; UPS transport locks fitted; IT removed or strapped; racks blocked; drains capped and traps drained.
- Humidity: two humidity indicator cards (30/40/50 %) inside the unit — one at the BEN Controller panel, one at the far rack — plus desiccant 1 kg per 10 m3 in sealed units; the indicators are photographed at set-down (Q-12).
- Shock: a tilt indicator and a 10 g shock indicator on the UPS frame and on rack R1; tripped indicators are recorded as an NCR and trigger a re-check of hold-downs and connections at site.
- BEN 5: forklift only from the long side with the cabinet empty of IT; the skid lifting label and CoG label fitted.

## 15. Change control
