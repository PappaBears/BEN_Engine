Result: the 20 ft module carries **two racks at up to 155 kW = 310 kW**, so the name **BEN 300** stands (rated 300 kW at 150 kW per rack). The Rev 2 decision's target (BEN 1000, 7 racks) does not fit, so the tier is **BEN 700** (5 × 140 kW), and `ben1000` loads as `ben700`.

## Notes

- Residual air: the W45 loop (40 °C supply) cannot cool room air, so the in-row fan coils run on an 18 °C trim loop from a chiller on the skid; the 20 kW per 300 mm fan coil is a design value, not a datasheet figure.
- Heat rejection: a W45 dry cooler needs design ambient + 8 K approach ≤ 45 °C; above a 37 °C design day (hot / arid) the skid needs adiabatic pre-cooling.
- NFPA 855: each lithium cabinet (32.6 kWh) is one group under the 50 kWh limit; cabinets stand side by side only with UL 9540A data for reduced spacing.
- NEC 110.26: the racks and CDUs face 932-1000 mm of aisle (condition 1, non-conductive liner panels opposite the rack zone); the 480 V gear backs onto the wall and has ≥ 1,200 mm in front (condition 2, grounded liner opposite).
