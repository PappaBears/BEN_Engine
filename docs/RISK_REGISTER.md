# BEN Engine — Risk Register (disclosed before pricing)

**Owner:** Schreiber AI compliance & operations. **Rule (from `web/kit/brand.json`):** risks are shown before prices. This register is the source for the product page's risk panel; the machine-readable extract is `web/assets/risks.json` and must be kept in step with this file.

How to read it: **Likelihood** L/M/H is for a unit operated per `docs/MAINTENANCE.md` over a 5-year life. **Consequence** is what the customer feels. **Covered by** is what BEN Engine does about it (design, monitoring, procedure, warranty or insurance). **Customer owns** is what stays with the buyer however good the box is. Severity in the JSON is our judgement of likelihood × consequence (low / medium / high).
