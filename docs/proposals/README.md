Nothing is hand-edited; regenerate after any catalog or config change.

## The 16 sections

1 Cover (proposal ID = hash of config + date + customer) · 2 Executive summary · 3 Mission profile and what it means · 4 The unit: sizing (from `Config.derived()`) · 5 Scope of supply by module, top-15 BOM lines, exclusions · 6 Price by option with confidence split, per kW, payment milestones 30/40/25/5 · 7 Schedule (weeks from order, from PROCESS_FLOW durations and BOM lead times) · 8 Drawings and documents · 9 Compliance pack (COMPLIANCE.md §11 row for the profile, §12 row for the climate) · 10 Risks disclosed up front (risks.json filtered by profile) · 11 Site requirements summary · 12 Acceptance: the 10 commissioning steps · 13 Warranty, maintenance and support · 14 Commercial terms summary (points to `docs/contracts/`) · 15 Assumptions and exclusions · 16 Signature block.

## Regenerate

```bash
cd software
python -m ben_engine.proposal --tier ben20 --profile healthcare --climate hot_humid --option managed \
  --customer "Acme Clinic" --site "Austin, TX" --date 2026-11-08 \
  --out ../docs/proposals/sample-ben20-healthcare.md --html ../docs/proposals/sample-ben20-healthcare.html \
  --fill-sow ../docs/contracts/SOW_TEMPLATE.md --out-sow ../docs/proposals/sample-ben20-healthcare-SOW-draft.md \
  --fill-change-order ../docs/contracts/CHANGE_ORDER.md --out-change-order ../docs/proposals/sample-ben20-healthcare-CHANGE_ORDER-draft.md \
  --field GOVERNING_STATE=Texas
```

Options: `hardware_only`, `hardware_controls`, `white_label` (`--brand`), `managed`, `modules` (`--subsystems power,cooling`). The HTML is self-contained (brand colours from `web/kit/brand.json`, inline SAI logo, dark on screen, light when printed, one section per printed page, "Schreiber AI — proposal" watermark). Screenshots: `node scripts/shoot_proposal.mjs` → `docs/media/screens/proposal-1.png`, `proposal-2.png`, `proposal-print.png`.

## Contract pre-fill

`fill_contract(template_path, proposal, extra_fields=None)` replaces `[FIELD_NAME]` tokens from `contract_fields(proposal)`; unknown fields are left in place and listed in `.unfilled`. The SOW and change-order drafts above were filled from the stub templates in `tests/fixtures/` because `docs/contracts/` did not exist when the samples were generated; re-run with the real templates once they land.
