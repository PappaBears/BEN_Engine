## About this document

This document tells you how to assemble one unit. Each task has these parts: the people you need, the time, the tools, the parts, the warnings, the steps and the checks.
Do the tasks in the order given. Do not start a task before the checks of the task before it are complete.
A **HOLD POINT** stops the work. Only the named qualified person can do the work after a hold point. Work does not continue until that person signs the form.
A **WITNESS POINT** needs the QC inspector present. The inspector signs the form.
The CTQ and CTP references point to the tables on the drawings and to `design/ctq.py`. The Q-forms are in `design/assembly/QC_FORMS.md`.

### Safety rules for all tasks

> **WARNING:** Electrical work can kill. Only a licensed electrician connects or disconnects power conductors. Lock out and tag out before you open an electrical enclosure.

> **WARNING:** Racks and cabinets are heavy. One rack has a mass of up to 1000 kg when loaded. Use the rack lifter. Do not stand under a lifted load.

> **WARNING:** Refrigerant and pressurised coolant can cause injury. Only a licensed HVAC technician opens a refrigerant circuit.

> **CAUTION:** Wear the PPE in T-15 for all tasks. Wear hearing protection when you cut or grind.

> **CAUTION:** Lithium batteries can start a fire if you damage them. Do not drop a battery module. Keep the battery documents with the unit.

## Task 01 — Receiving inspection

**Personnel:** 2 workers, 1 QC inspector  
**Time:** 4 h  
**Tools:** T-01 Steel tape 8 m; T-09 Forklift 2.5 t or crane with spreader; T-14 Label printer; T-15 PPE: hard hat, safety glasses, gloves, steel-toe boots, hearing protection  
**Parts:** SITE-FORKLIFT (Forklift / tilt-bed delivery and set for Micro skid) x 1  


> **CAUTION:** Do not accept a damaged enclosure. A bent corner post or a cracked corner casting makes the unit unsafe to lift.

1. Compare the delivery note with the purchase order and the parts list.
2. Unload the cabinet with the forklift. Lift only at the skid fork pockets.
3. Put the enclosure on level blocks at the work area. Make sure there is 1.5 m of free space on all sides.
4. Examine all outer surfaces for dents, cracks, holes and corrosion. Mark each defect with chalk. Take a photo of each defect.
5. Read the rating label of the cabinet. Record the model and the IP rating.
6. Open the doors. Examine the floor and the walls from inside. Look for light through the walls. Look for water marks.
7. Measure the external length, the width and the height. Measure the two floor diagonals.
8. Open each pallet of parts. Count the parts against the parts list. Examine each part for transport damage.
9. Put a label with the item code on each pallet. Store the parts in the dry area.
10. Put the electrical parts and the batteries in the locked store.

**Check:**

- [CTQ-A1] External dimensions 1200 x 1000 x 1800 mm, each +0/-6 mm. Diagonals within 10 mm. Record on form Q-02.
- [CTQ-A2] No water ingress after 3 min hose test. Record on form Q-03.
- All parts counted. All defects photographed. Delivery note signed. Record on form Q-01.


## Task 02 — Cabinet preparation: cutouts for the air conditioners

**Personnel:** 2 workers  
**Time:** 6 h  
**Tools:** T-01 Steel tape 8 m; T-04 Cordless drill, step drill, hole saws; T-05 Angle grinder with cutting and grinding discs; T-07 Sealant gun, caulking tools; T-15 PPE: hard hat, safety glasses, gloves, steel-toe boots, hearing protection  
**Parts:** SVC-MODS-MAT (Container modification materials per opening (frame steel, flashing, louvers, primer)) x 1; ENV-MICRO-HEATER (Anti-condensation heater 100 W with thermostat + replacement IP55 door gasket (Micro cabinet))) x 1  


> **CAUTION:** The cabinet is rated IP55. Each cutout must get the gasket of the air conditioner. Do not make a cutout that is larger than the template.

1. Remove the side panels of the cabinet.
2. Put the cutting template of the air conditioner CLG-CABAC on each side panel at the positions on A-001. There are 2 units.
3. Mark the cutout and the bolt holes. Drill the bolt holes. Cut the opening with the angle grinder.
4. Grind the edges smooth. Apply primer to all cut edges.
5. Drill the gland holes for the power entry and the fibre entry in the rear panel at z 600.
6. Fit the anti-condensation heater ENV-MICRO-HEATER at the bottom of the cabinet. Fit the new door gasket.
7. Fit the side panels again.

**Check:**

- [CTQ-A2] No water ingress after 3 min hose test. Record on form Q-03.
- All cutouts match the template. All cut edges primed. Record on form Q-02.

## Task 04 — Cooling units, refrigerant and coolant piping

**Personnel:** 2 workers, 1 licensed HVAC technician, 1 QC inspector (witness)  
**Time:** 1.0 days (8 h)  
**Tools:** T-01 Steel tape 8 m; T-02 Spirit level 1.8 m / laser level; T-03 Calibrated torque wrench 20-200 N·m (cal. within 12 months); T-08 Pallet jack / rack lifter, 1000 kg; T-09 Forklift 2.5 t or crane with spreader; T-11 Refractometer, vacuum pump, micron gauge, nitrogen set (HVAC technician); T-15 PPE: hard hat, safety glasses, gloves, steel-toe boots, hearing protection  
**Parts:** CLG-CABAC (Blue e+ wall-mounted enclosure cooling unit 5.8 kW, 400/480 V 3-ph) x 2; CLG-CONDPUMP (Condensate pump per DX unit) x 2; STR-FASTENERS (Fastener & hardware kit (self-drilling TEK screws, rivets, Unistrut, anchors)), zinc flake) x 8  


> **WARNING:** HOLD POINT. Only a licensed HVAC technician (EPA 608 / F-gas) brazes, pressure tests, evacuates and charges a refrigerant circuit. Workers do the mechanical work only.


> **WARNING:** Refrigerant R-454B is mildly flammable (A2L). No open flame near an open circuit. Ventilate the work area.

1. Lift each cabinet air conditioner CLG-CABAC onto its cutout. There are 2 units. Fit the gasket. Fit the bolts from inside.
2. Fit the condensate drain hose from each unit to the drain spigot at the skid.
3. HVAC technician: check the factory charge. The units are pre-charged and sealed. No brazing is needed.
4. Pour 1 L of water in each drain tray. Make sure the water clears in less than 60 seconds.
5. Fit the tag label on each unit (CU1, CND1, PU1 ...).

**Check:**

- [CTP-M1] Nitrogen purge while brazing; vacuum <= 500 micron held 30 min; charge per nameplate. HOLD POINT. Record on form Q-06.
- [CTQ-M1] Refrigerant: 1.1 x design pressure 24 h, drop <= 1 %; water: 1.5 x working pressure 2 h, no drop. WITNESS POINT. Record on form Q-06.
- [CTQ-M2] Clears in < 60 s; no pooling. Record on form Q-06.


## Task 05 — Electrical installation

**Personnel:** 2 workers, 1 licensed electrician, 1 QC inspector (witness)  
**Time:** 2.0 days (16 h)  
**Tools:** T-01 Steel tape 8 m; T-03 Calibrated torque wrench 20-200 N·m (cal. within 12 months); T-04 Cordless drill, step drill, hole saws; T-08 Pallet jack / rack lifter, 1000 kg; T-10 True-RMS multimeter, insulation tester 1000 V, earth tester; T-14 Label printer; T-15 PPE: hard hat, safety glasses, gloves, steel-toe boots, hearing protection; T-16 Rack lifter / stair climber for 100 kg modules  
**Parts:** PWR-LOADCTR (QO 100 A 20-space outdoor (NEMA 3R) load center with 50 A 2P main for Micro) x 1; PWR-PDU (NetShelter metered rack PDU 0U 5.0 kW 208 V 30 A, L6-30P, (36) C13 (6) C19) x 2; PWR-FEEDER (Feeder cable, conduit, lugs, breaker-to-UPS-to-PDU distribution (per ampere of service breaker)), pre-cut and labelled) x 1; PWR-GROUNDBAR (Telecom grounding busbar kit 1/4 x 4 x 12 in with insulators/brackets) x 1; PWR-RCPT (Rack PDU receptacle IEC 60309 30/32 A with box and labelled pigtail (A + B per rack)) x 2; CTL-LABELS (Label printer cartridges, cable labels, engraved phenolic tags (lot)) x 1; PWR-UPS-SRT6K (Smart-UPS SRT 6 kVA RM 208 V online double-conversion), with network card) x 2; PWR-UPS-SRT6K-XBP (Smart-UPS SRT 192 V external battery pack (for 10 min at 6 kW)) x 1  


> **WARNING:** HOLD POINT. Only a licensed electrician lands power conductors, connects the service, and energises. Workers mount enclosures, pull labelled cables and plug in labelled connectors after the electrician signs.


> **WARNING:** The battery cabinet has stored energy. Keep the battery disconnect open until the electrician completes the tests.


> **WARNING:** Lock out and tag out the service disconnect before you work in any enclosure.

1. Fit the rack UPS modules PWR-UPS-SRT6K and the battery PWR-UPS-SRT6K-XBP in rack R1 at the bottom U positions on A-002. Use the rack lifter T-16 for each module.
2. Mount the service entrance box PWR-LOADCTR on the rear of the cabinet at z 600 to 1200.
3. Mount the EPO station FS-EPO-MICRO beside the door at z 1200.
4. Pull the labelled cables PWR-FEEDER in the power tray. Keep power cables in TR-P only. Keep 300 mm from the data tray. Cross at 90 degrees.
5. Fit two rack PDUs PWR-PDU in each rack. Plug each PDU into its receptacle A or B.
6. Fit the ground bar PWR-GROUNDBAR in the service section. Connect the container frame bond at two places. Connect the tray ground, the rack grounds and each cabinet frame.
7. Electrician: land all conductors. Torque each lug to the value on the device label. Record each value.
8. Electrician: test insulation resistance of each feeder at 1000 V. Test phase rotation at each panel.

> **WARNING:** WITNESS POINT. Call the QC inspector for the ground resistance and the termination torque record.

9. Electrician: energise the service section. Measure the voltage at each panel. Start the UPS per the manufacturer start-up procedure.
10. Press the EPO. Make sure all IT power and all cooling units go off in less than 1 second. Reset the EPO.
11. Fit the tag label on each breaker, each cable end and each receptacle.

**Check:**

## Task 06 — BEN Controller panel, BEN Pods and transmitters

**Personnel:** 2 workers, 1 controls technician, 1 licensed electrician (panel feeder)

> **CAUTION:** Static electricity can damage a Pod or an I/O module. Touch the rack frame before you touch a circuit board. Do not open a Pod: there is nothing to solder or set inside. A Pod with a broken seal is returned, not repaired.


> **WARNING:** The controls panel is fed from PNL-M. Only a licensed electrician lands the panel feeder. Lock out the feeder before you work inside the panel.


> **NOTE:** Sensor names on the labels must match spec/PROTOCOL.md exactly. The software finds each sensor by its name.

1. Fit the BEN Controls Lite shelf CTL-ENCL in the rack at the U position on A-002. Fit the RevPi CTL-REVPI with the DIO and AIO modules, the 24 VDC supply CTL-PSU-QUINT and the switch CTL-SWITCH-LITE on the shelf DIN rail.
2. Electrician: land the panel feeder from PNL-M and the ground. Land 24 VDC to the field terminal strip: one fused 24 VDC branch per Pod E, one per transmitter, one for the leak controller. Torque the terminals to the value on the label (T-17).
3. Mount the 3 Pods CTL-POD. Pods P1 to P2 go on the top rail of each rack: odd numbers on the cold face, even numbers on the hot face. The positions are:
   - P1 at x 240, y 710, z 1050
   - P2 at x 240, y 40, z 1050
   - P3 at x 200, y 20, z 1250
4. Fit the probe string CTL-PT1000 of each Pod: three inlet probes on the front door of the rack at z 300, 700 and 1100 (50 mm in front of the door face) on the cold-face Pod, one outlet probe at the rear at z 700 on the hot-face Pod. Plug the M12 connector into the Pod. Fix each probe with its clip.
5. Label each probe at the sensor and at the Pod: T_in_r1_0.3, T_in_r1_0.7, T_in_r1_1.1, T_out_r1, and so on.
6. Fit T_supply at the cooling unit outlet and T_return at the inlet. Connect them to the room Pod E.
7. Connect the room Pod E to the RevPi with the RS-485 cable (A, B, shield) and 24 VDC.
8. The room Pod E carries the RH sensor. Mount the Pod at the room centre position on C-001 if the layout shows it there. Label RH_room.
9. Route the dP tubes of the room Pod E: one to the rack front and one to the rack rear. Label dP_aisle.
10. Connect the leak rope CTL-LEAK to the room Pod E leak input. Fit the end-of-line terminator.
11. Fit the door contact CTL-DOOR on the door at z 1000. Wire it to a DI on the panel. Label door_main.
12. Connect the utility meter pulse output to a DI on the panel (meter1, pulse_kwh).
13. Connect the Modbus TCP cables from the UPS, the cooling units and the PDUs to the switch CTL-SWITCH-LITE. Set the switch to the controls VLAN only. No route to the internet.
14. Connect the dry contacts from the fire releasing panel and the EPO to the panel DI. These are monitor contacts only. Life safety stays hard-wired.
15. Energise the cabinet. Make sure the 24 VDC supply shows DC OK and the RevPi boots.
16. Plug the border router radio CTL-RCP into the RevPi USB port.
17. Start `ben_engine` on the laptop T-12. Run `python -m ben_engine.commissioning --stage provisioning-check`. Make sure all 3 Pods join the mesh, show attestation OK and a valid firmware signature. A Pod that does not attest is replaced, not re-flashed in the field.
18. Open the containment doors. Send the tare command to dP_aisle. Close the doors.
19. Put the probes of rack R1 in the ice-point bath, then the 40 C bath. Enter the reference values in the calibration tool. The coefficients are stored in the Pod.
20. Wet a finger and touch the leak rope at each 1 m. Make sure the controller reports leak = 1 within 10 seconds.
21. Open and close the door. Make sure door_main changes within 2 seconds.
22. Stop `ben_engine` on the RevPi. Make sure the watchdog relay drops within 5 seconds and the cooling units keep running on local control. Restart.

- [CTQ-K2] 100 % match; label at sensor and at Pod / panel terminal. Record on form Q-08.
- [CTQ-K3] Within +/-0.25 C after correction; cal_id stored in the Pod (Secure Vault NVM). WITNESS POINT. Record on form Q-08.
- [CTQ-K4] dP reads 0 +/-0.4 Pa after tare; door = 1 within 2 s. Record on form Q-08.

- [CTP-K2] Cooling units fall back to local control and the watchdog relay drops within 5 s; alarm logged on return; no IT power affected. WITNESS POINT. Record on form Q-08.

## Task 08 — Final QC and pre-ship test

**Personnel:** 1 QC inspector, 1 controls technician, 1 electrician  
**Time:** 1.0 days (8 h)  
**Tools:** T-01 Steel tape 8 m; T-02 Spirit level 1.8 m / laser level; T-10 True-RMS multimeter, insulation tester 1000 V, earth tester; T-12 Laptop with ben_engine software and USB-serial cable; T-13 Smoke pencil, manometer, paper strips; T-15 PPE: hard hat, safety glasses, gloves, steel-toe boots, hearing protection  


> **NOTE:** The pre-ship test proves the unit before it leaves the factory. The commissioning script repeats part of this test at the site.

1. Walk the unit with the drawings. Compare each tag label with A-002 and C-001. Correct any label.
2. Make sure the door opens 120 degrees and the rails are plumb.
3. Close all doors and panels. Energise the unit from the test supply.
4. Connect the load bank to the rack PDUs. Apply 50 % of the IT load. Run for 1 hour.
5. Run `python -m ben_engine.commissioning --stage pre-ship` on the laptop. The script reads all sensors and all equipment.
6. Make sure every rack inlet is between 18 C and 27 C. Make sure all cooling units share the load within 15 %.
7. Apply 100 % of the IT load. Run for 2 hours. Record the supply, the return and the inlet temperatures every 10 minutes.
8. Open the utility breaker. Make sure the UPS carries the load. Make sure the runtime is more than the design minutes. Close the breaker.
9. Press the EPO. Make sure all IT power and cooling go off in less than 1 second. Reset.
10. Remove the load bank. Record the energy meter reading.
11. Print the pre-ship report from the script. Attach the report to form Q-10.

**Check:**

- [CTQ-B6] 100 % of tags present, laminated, 10 mm text minimum. Record on form Q-10.
- [CTQ-M5] Rack inlet 18-27 C (ASHRAE A2); all units share load within 15 %. Record on form Q-10.
- [CTQ-E3] All IT power off < 1 s; recorded on Q-10. WITNESS POINT. Record on form Q-10.
- UPS runtime at full IT load >= 10 min. Pre-ship report signed. Record on form Q-10.
