## About this document

This document tells you how to assemble one unit. Each task has these parts: the people you need, the time, the tools, the parts, the warnings, the steps and the checks.
Do the tasks in the order given. Do not start a task before the checks of the task before it are complete.
A **HOLD POINT** stops the work. Only the named qualified person can do the work after a hold point. Work does not continue until that person signs the form.
A **WITNESS POINT** needs the QC inspector present. The inspector signs the form.
The CTQ and CTP references point to the tables on the drawings and to `design/ctq.py`. The Q-forms are in `design/assembly/QC_FORMS.md`.

## Task 01 — Receiving inspection

**Personnel:** 2 workers, 1 QC inspector  
**Time:** 4 h  
**Tools:** T-01 Steel tape 8 m; T-09 Forklift 2.5 t or crane with spreader; T-14 Label printer; T-15 PPE: hard hat, safety glasses, gloves, steel-toe boots, hearing protection  
**Parts:** SITE-CRANE (Mobile crane with operator, half day, set container on pad) x 1  


> **CAUTION:** Do not accept a damaged enclosure. A bent corner post or a cracked corner casting makes the unit unsafe to lift.

1. Compare the delivery note with the purchase order and the parts list.
2. Unload the enclosure with the forklift or the crane. Lift only at the four corner castings.
3. Put the enclosure on level blocks at the work area. Make sure there is 1.5 m of free space on all sides.
4. Examine all outer surfaces for dents, cracks, holes and corrosion. Mark each defect with chalk. Take a photo of each defect.
5. Read the CSC plate. Record the owner code, the serial number and the date of the last inspection.
6. Open the doors. Examine the floor and the walls from inside. Look for light through the walls. Look for water marks.
7. Measure the external length, the width and the height. Measure the two floor diagonals.
8. Open each pallet of parts. Count the parts against the parts list. Examine each part for transport damage.
9. Put a label with the item code on each pallet. Store the parts in the dry area.
10. Put the electrical parts and the batteries in the locked store.

**Check:**

- [CTQ-A1] External dimensions 2991 x 2438 x 2896 mm, each +0/-6 mm. Diagonals within 10 mm. Record on form Q-02.
- [CTQ-A2] No cracks, no weld repair without report; CSC plate date valid. Record on form Q-01.
- All parts counted. All defects photographed. Delivery note signed. Record on form Q-01.


## Task 02 — Container preparation: cutouts and reinforcement

**Personnel:** 2 workers, 1 certified welder  
**Time:** 2.0 days (16 h)  
**Tools:** T-01 Steel tape 8 m; T-04 Cordless drill, step drill, hole saws; T-05 Angle grinder with cutting and grinding discs; T-06 Welding set (certified welder only); T-07 Sealant gun, caulking tools; T-15 PPE: hard hat, safety glasses, gloves, steel-toe boots, hearing protection  
**Parts:** SVC-MODS-MAT (Container modification materials per opening (frame steel, flashing, louvers, primer)) x 5; SVC-MODS-MAT (Container modification materials per opening (frame steel, flashing, louvers, primer)) x 2  


> **WARNING:** Grinding and welding make sparks and fumes. Keep a fire extinguisher at the work area. Remove all flammable material 10 m around.


> **WARNING:** HOLD POINT. Only a certified welder (AWS D1.1 or ISO 9606-1) welds on the container. The welder signs form Q-02.

1. Mark the position of each cutout on the outside of the container. Use the dimensions on A-001 and A-003. The cutouts are:
   - 2 x line-set roof curbs 300 x 200 per condenser position (see A-001)
   - 1 x power entry 150 x 150 in the blind end wall behind the service section, z 1800
   - 1 x fibre / controls entry 100 x 100 in the blind end wall, z 2200
   - 1 x floor drain 60 mm hole at x 300, y 1400 (see A-003)
   - 1 x condensate drain exit 40 mm in the rack wall at floor level, x 2400

> **CAUTION:** Do not cut within 300 mm of a corner post. Do not cut the top side rail or the bottom side rail.

2. Measure each mark again from a second reference. Make sure the two measurements agree within 5 mm.
3. Drill a 10 mm hole at each corner of each cutout.
4. Cut each opening with the angle grinder. Cut from hole to hole. Remove the cut piece.
5. Grind all cut edges smooth. Remove all burrs.
6. Welder: fit the reinforcement frame SVC-MODS-MAT around each opening. Tack the frame in four places. Measure the frame for square.
7. Welder: weld the frame fully to the shell. Make a continuous fillet weld on the outside and the inside.
8. Welder: examine each weld. Look for cracks, undercut and porosity. Repair any defect.
9. Clean each weld with a wire brush. Apply zinc-rich primer within 4 hours. Apply the topcoat the next day.
10. Weld the four tray bosses to the top side rail at the positions on S-001.
11. Weld the two seismic bosses to the top side rail if the seismic option applies (S-001 Detail B).
12. Record the welder name, the licence number and the date on form Q-02.

**Check:**

- [CTP-A1] Frame 50x50x5 fully welded, no undercut > 0.5 mm; primed within 4 h. HOLD POINT: welder signs. Record on form Q-02.
- Each cutout position within 5 mm of A-001. No cutout within 300 mm of a corner post. Record on form Q-02.
- Primer applied to 100 % of cut edges and welds. Photo of each frame. Record on form Q-02.


## Task 03 — Insulation, vapour barrier and liner

**Personnel:** 3 workers  
**Time:** 2.0 days (16 h)  
**Tools:** T-01 Steel tape 8 m; T-04 Cordless drill, step drill, hole saws; T-07 Sealant gun, caulking tools; T-13 Smoke pencil, manometer, paper strips; T-15 PPE: hard hat, safety glasses, gloves, steel-toe boots, hearing protection  
**Parts:** ENV-PIR (Polyiso (PIR) foil-faced board 2 in x 4 ft x 8 ft, R-13 (two layers = 100 mm)) x 12; ENV-HAT (Steel hat channel / furring 7/8 in x 10 ft)) x 1; ENV-LINER (Ribbed 29-ga galvanized steel liner panel 3 ft x 8 ft) x 13; ENV-SEAL (Fire-rated sealant / low-expansion PU foam) x 8; ENV-SEAL (Fire-rated sealant / low-expansion PU foam)) x 1; ENV-DOORSEAL (EPDM bulb door seal 20 mm, self-adhesive, 10 m roll (secondary seal on cargo doors)) x 1; ENV-FLOOR-PIR (Floor build-up: 80 mm PIR + 18 mm plywood, per m2 (under anti-static vinyl, A-003 Detail 2)) x 3; ENV-FLOOR (Anti-static vinyl floor tile over container plywood floor) x 1; ENV-DRAIN (Floor drain 50 mm with trap and exterior cap, stainless) x 1; ENV-ROOFBOOT (EPDM roof boot + butyl tape + clamp band for line-set / pipe curb) x 3  


> **CAUTION:** PIR dust irritates the eyes and the skin. Wear safety glasses and gloves when you cut PIR.


> **NOTE:** The liner is the vapour barrier. Every joint and every penetration must be sealed. A gap lets warm air reach the cold steel and makes water.

1. Clean the inside of the shell. Remove all oil, dust and loose paint.
2. Apply the closed-cell foam ENV-SEAL to the corner posts, the door frame and the floor cross-member ends inside the shell.
3. Fit the first PIR board ENV-PIR at the blind end wall, bottom corner. Apply adhesive in strips 200 mm apart. Push the board into the corrugation.
4. Fit the next boards. Stagger the joints by 600 mm. Push each board tightly against the board before it.
5. Fit four pins per square metre through each board into the corrugation crests.
6. Cut the boards around each reinforcement frame. Leave a gap of not more than 3 mm. Fill the gap with foam sealant.
7. Tape all board joints with foil tape.
8. Fit the roof boards the same way. Support each board with a prop until the adhesive is set.
9. Fit the floor: put the 80 mm PIR between the floor channel positions marked on S-001. Lay the 18 mm plywood on the PIR. Screw the plywood to the container floor at 300 mm pitch.
10. Fit the floor drain ENV-DRAIN at x 300, y 1400. Make sure the drain is the lowest point. Fall 1:100 toward the drain.
11. Fit the liner panels ENV-LINER on the walls. Start at the blind end. Overlap each panel 25 mm. Apply a continuous sealant bead ENV-SEAL in each overlap.
12. Fit the liner on the roof. Fit the corner trims. Seal all trims.
13. Fit the EPDM roof boots ENV-ROOFBOOT on each roof curb. Clamp the band. Seal with butyl tape.
14. Fit 25 mm PIR and liner on the inside of each door. Fit the EPDM bulb seal ENV-DOORSEAL around the door frame.
15. Lay the anti-static vinyl ENV-FLOOR on the plywood. Weld the seams. Fit the coved skirting.
16. Test the envelope with the smoke pencil at 10 joints. Hold the smoke 20 mm from the joint. The smoke must not move.

**Check:**

- [CTQ-C1] 100 mm +5/-0; no gap > 3 mm; joints taped. Record on form Q-03.
- [CTQ-C2] Continuous sealant bead; no smoke movement. Record on form Q-03.
- [CTQ-C3] Paper held at every point; no light visible from inside. Record on form Q-03.
- [CTQ-C4] 20 mm closed-cell foam, no bare steel inside the envelope. Record on form Q-03.
- [CTP-C1] Fall >= 1:100 toward drain; water test clears in < 60 s. Record on form Q-03.

**Personnel:** 2 workers, 1 QC inspector (witness)  
**Time:** 1.0 days (8 h)  
**Tools:** T-01 Steel tape 8 m; T-02 Spirit level 1.8 m / laser level; T-03 Calibrated torque wrench 20-200 N·m (cal. within 12 months); T-04 Cordless drill, step drill, hole saws; T-15 PPE: hard hat, safety glasses, gloves, steel-toe boots, hearing protection

> **NOTE:** The floor channels carry the rack feet. The channels must bear on the plywood over the cross members, not on insulation.

1. Mark the two channel centre lines on the floor. See S-001 for the y positions. Mark the channel length 2100 mm from the door-end liner.
2. Mark the position of each floor cross member on the channel line. The cross members are at about 300 mm pitch. Find them with a magnet or by drilling a pilot hole.
3. Cut the channels STR-FLOORCHAN to length. Remove the burrs. Apply primer to the cut ends.
4. Put the first channel on the line. Drill 13 mm holes through the channel web, the plywood and the cross-member flange at each cross member.
5. Fit an M12 bolt STR-FASTENERS in each hole, bolt head up, washer under the head and under the nut.
6. Put the laser level on the channel. Measure the height every 600 mm. Put steel shims STR-FASTENERS under the channel where the height is low.

> **CAUTION:** Use steel shims only. Do not use plywood or plastic shims.

7. Do the same for the second channel. Make sure the two channels are parallel within 2 mm.

> **WARNING:** WITNESS POINT. Call the QC inspector before you torque the bolts.

8. Torque each M12 bolt to 85 N·m with the calibrated torque wrench. Put a paint stripe across the nut and the channel after each bolt.
9. Mark the tray hanger positions on the ceiling liner at 1500 mm pitch. The data tray TR-D is at y 1370 to 1670. The power tray TR-P is at y 1770 to 2070.
10. Fit the hangers STR-FASTENERS to the tray bosses with M10 rod. Fit the trays STR-TRAY and STR-TRAY at z 2250 (underside).
11. Fit the tray ground bonding jumper at each tray joint.
12. Lay the leak rope CTL-LEAK on the floor around the perimeter 100 mm from the walls. Fix the rope with clips every 500 mm. Leave the ends at the controller position.

**Check:**

- [CTP-S1] 85 N·m +/-10 %; mark each bolt with paint stripe. WITNESS POINT. Record on form Q-04.
- [CTQ-S1] Within 3 mm over the full row length; shim with steel only. Record on form Q-04.
- Tray underside at 2250 +/-10 mm. Trays bonded. Leak rope continuous, ends labelled. Record on form Q-04.

> **WARNING:** HOLD POINT. Only a licensed HVAC technician (EPA 608 / F-gas) brazes, pressure tests, evacuates and charges a refrigerant circuit. Workers do the mechanical work only.
