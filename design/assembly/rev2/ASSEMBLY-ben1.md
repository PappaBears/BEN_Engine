## About this document

Schreiber AI builds BEN 1 in house, on a bench. This document tells you how to build one unit of either form. Steps marked (rack10) or (cab12) apply to that form only.
A **HOLD POINT** stops the work. Only the named person can do the work after a hold point. A **WITNESS POINT** needs the QC inspector present.
The CTQ references point to the tables on the drawings and to `design/ctq.py`. The Q-forms are in `design/assembly/QC_FORMS.md`.

### Safety rules for all tasks

> **WARNING:** Do not open a UPS. Do not open a device power supply. Only the listed cords connect power.

> **CAUTION:** The cab12 cabinet has a mass of 75 kg empty. Two persons lift it. Use the bench lift table.

> **CAUTION:** Lithium batteries are inside the UPS. Do not drop the UPS. Keep the UPS documents with the unit.


## Task 01 — Unpack and receiving inspection

**Personnel:** 1 technician, 1 QC inspector  
**Time:** 1 h  
**Tools:** T-01 Steel tape 8 m; T-14 Label printer; T-15 PPE: hard hat, safety glasses, gloves, steel-toe boots, hearing protection  

1. Compare the delivery notes with the order and the A-001 device list.
2. Open each carton. Examine each item for transport damage. Take a photo of each defect.
3. Read the model label of the rack or the cabinet. Record the model and the serial number.
4. Put a label with the BEN 1 serial number on the rack frame.

**Check:**

- [CTQ-A1] APC NetShelter CX Mini 12U (AR4000MVX431); 688 x 952 x 744 mm +/-5. Record on form Q-01.


## Task 02 — Rack the devices

**Personnel:** 1 technician  
**Time:** 2 h  
**Tools:** T-01 Steel tape 8 m; T-03 Calibrated torque wrench 20-200 N·m (cal. within 12 months); T-15 PPE: hard hat, safety glasses, gloves, steel-toe boots, hearing protection  
**Parts:** STR-MINIRACK-10 (10-inch 8U desk / shelf mini rack, aluminium + acrylic (DeskPi RackMate T1), 281 W x 200 D x 451 H mm); STR-MINICAB-12 (APC NetShelter CX Mini 12U sound-damped office cabinet with PDU (744 H x 688 W x 952 D mm, 75 kg, 18.5 dB broadband noise reduction)); CMP-SW-MINI (UniFi Switch Lite 8 PoE, 8 x GbE, 4 x PoE+ (52 W), managed) x 1; CTL-EDGE-MINI (Fanless edge computer for the BEN Care agent (OnLogic BlastShield CL210, dual LAN) — omit when BEN Care runs on the customer's own box) x 1  


> **CAUTION:** Fit the heavy items low. Do not put a device on top of another device without a shelf.

1. Put the rack or the cabinet on the bench. Level it.
2. Fit the shelves and the devices in the U positions of drawing A-001, from the bottom up.
3. (rack10) Fit the 10-inch PDU in U1 and the switch in U2.
4. (rack10) Fit the AI boxes on their shelves. Fit the edge computer at the top.
5. (cab12) Fit the UPS in U1 and U2. Then fit the switch, the NAS and the 2U server.
6. (cab12) Fit the edge computer and the patch panel at the top.
7. (cab12) Fit blanking panels in each open U.
8. Pull each shelf and each device by hand. Nothing must move.

**Check:**

- [CTQ-A2] Every device in its U; cage nuts / shelf screws tight; nothing loose on a shelf; blanking panels in open U (cab12). Record on form Q-05.


## Task 03 — Cable

**Personnel:** 1 technician  
**Time:** 1.5 h  
**Tools:** T-14 Label printer; T-15 PPE: hard hat, safety glasses, gloves, steel-toe boots, hearing protection  
**Parts:** CTL-CAT6A (Cat6A UTP patch cable 10 ft) x 1; CTL-LABELS (Label printer cartridges, cable labels, engraved phenolic tags (lot)) x 1  

1. Connect each device to the switch with a labelled patch cable.
2. Connect the edge computer and the uplink port of the switch. Label the uplink port CUSTOMER.
3. Connect each device power cord to the PDU or to a UPS outlet. Do not use a power strip or an extension cord.
4. Dress the cables on the rear rail with hook-and-loop straps. Keep the air path at the rear clear.

**Check:**

- Every cable labelled at both ends. No power strip, no extension cord. Record on form Q-07.


## Task 04 — UPS

**Personnel:** 1 technician, 1 QC inspector (witness)  
**Time:** 1 h  
**Tools:** T-10 True-RMS multimeter, insulation tester 1000 V, earth tester; T-15 PPE: hard hat, safety glasses, gloves, steel-toe boots, hearing protection  
**Parts:** PWR-UPS-MINI-1000 (Smart-UPS Li-ion short depth 1000 VA / 800 W 120 V, 2U rack/tower, line-interactive, SmartConnect (NEMA 5-15P)) x 1; PWR-UPS-MINI-2200 (Smart-UPS Li-ion 2200 VA / 1920 W 120 V, 2U rackmount, line-interactive, SmartConnect (NEMA 5-20P; 6 x 5-15R + 2 x 5-20R)) x 1  


> **WARNING:** HOLD POINT. Use only listed cord-connected equipment. Do not change a plug or a cord.

1. (rack10) Put the UPS PWR-UPS-MINI-1000 beside the rack in tower position. Connect the PDU cord to a battery outlet.
2. (cab12) Connect the cords of the devices to the battery outlets of the UPS PWR-UPS-MINI-2200.
3. Connect the UPS network port to the switch. Connect the UPS USB cable to the edge computer.
4. Measure the resistance from the plug ground pin to the rack frame and to each chassis.
5. Connect the UPS plug to the bench test receptacle. Start the UPS. Run the UPS self-test.

**Check:**

- [CTP-E1] Every power item carries a listing mark; no splices, no extension cords, no daisy-chained power strips. HOLD POINT. Record on form Q-07.
- [CTQ-E1] <= 0.1 ohm from the UPS plug ground pin to the rack frame and every chassis. Record on form Q-07.


## Task 05 — BEN Pods and the BEN Care agent

**Personnel:** 1 controls technician, 1 QC inspector (witness)  
**Time:** 1.5 h  
**Tools:** T-12 Laptop with ben_engine software and USB-serial cable; T-17 Ferrule crimper, torque screwdriver 0.5-2.5 N·m (controls terminals), RS-485 / loop test meter; T-15 PPE: hard hat, safety glasses, gloves, steel-toe boots, hearing protection  
**Parts:** CTL-POD (BEN Pod v1 assembled and provisioned (MGM240P Thread module w/ Secure Vault High, 4x Pt1000 3-wire 24-bit front end, SHT45, 2x dry contact, leak-rope input, 2x isolated 4-20 mA; Pod E adds Ethernet/PoE PD, RS-485, 2x relay) - make item, per unit) x 2  


> **CAUTION:** Static electricity can damage a Pod. Touch the rack frame before you touch a Pod.

1. Attach Pod P1 to the front rail at the height on drawing C-001. P1 measures the inlet air.
2. Attach Pod P2 to the rear rail at the height on drawing C-001. P2 measures the exhaust air.
3. Run the calibration of the two Pods in the ice-point and 40 C baths.
4. Start the edge computer. Enrol the BEN Care agent with the unit serial number.
5. Run `python -m ben_engine.commissioning --stage pre-ship`. Read the mesh report.

**Check:**

- [CTQ-K1] Pods at the drawn U positions +/-1U; probes 20-50 mm from the rail. Record on form Q-08.
- [CTQ-K3] Within +/-0.25 C after correction; cal_id in the Pod. WITNESS POINT. Record on form Q-08.
- [CTQ-K5] Both Pods attested; edge agent online in BEN Care; unprovisioned Pod rejected. WITNESS POINT. Record on form Q-08.


## Task 06 — Burn-in 24 h

**Personnel:** 1 technician, 1 QC inspector  
**Time:** 3.1 days (25 h)  
**Tools:** T-18 Bench scale 200 kg, sound level meter (class 2, A-weighted), clamp meter; T-19 Burn-in load script and laptop on the bench network; T-15 PPE: hard hat, safety glasses, gloves, steel-toe boots, hearing protection  


> **NOTE:** HOLD POINT. The unit does not ship before the 24 h burn-in passes.

1. Start the burn-in script. It loads each device to full power.
2. Read the UPS load and the input current with the clamp meter. Record them.
3. Run the burn-in for 24 hours. The script logs the Pod inlet and exhaust every minute.
4. Measure the noise at 1 m in front of the unit with the sound level meter.
5. Pull the UPS plug at full load. Measure the runtime until the shutdown signal. Put the plug back.
6. Stop the script. Attach the burn-in report to form Q-10.

**Check:**

- [CTQ-E2] UPS load <= 80 % of 1920 W; input current <= 12 A (NEC 210.19/210.20 continuous). WITNESS POINT. Record on form Q-07.
- [CTQ-E3] Self-test pass; runtime >= 9 min at burn-in load; graceful shutdown signal reaches the hosts. WITNESS POINT. Record on form Q-10.
- [CTQ-T1] 24 h without fault; exhaust - inlet <= 10 K; noise at 1 m <= 45 dBA (sound meter, A-weighted). HOLD POINT. Record on form Q-10.


## Task 07 — Pack

**Personnel:** 1 technician  
**Time:** 1 h  
**Tools:** T-18 Bench scale 200 kg, sound level meter (class 2, A-weighted), clamp meter; T-14 Label printer; T-15 PPE: hard hat, safety glasses, gloves, steel-toe boots, hearing protection  

1. Weigh the unit. Record the mass.
2. Make sure the data plate band, the nameplate and the QR label are on the unit.
3. (cab12) Lock the door. Tape the keys inside the carton lid.
4. Put the unit in the shipping carton with the foam blocks.
5. Put the site sheet I-001, the burn-in report and the quick-start card in the carton.
6. Close and label the carton with the serial number and the lithium battery mark.

**Check:**

- [CTQ-A3] Mass 133 kg +10 % max; data plate band, nameplate and QR fitted; serial recorded. Record on form Q-10.
- [CTQ-I1] Site sheet I-001 in the carton; the installer and the customer sign CTQ-I1 at the site. Record on form Q-12.
