## 4. Working space and the aisle-wall liner (EN-001)

NEC Table 110.26(A)(1): 0-150 V to ground needs 914 mm under conditions 1, 2 and 3; 151-600 V needs 914 mm (condition 1: no live or grounded parts opposite), 1,067 mm (condition 2: grounded parts opposite) or 1,219 mm (condition 3). A galvanised steel liner bonded to the module frame is a grounded surface. Rev 2 puts ENV-LINER-NC (non-conductive FRP, ASTM E84 Class A, on FRT plywood, nylon fasteners, no metal trim) on the aisle wall of every 480 V module tier and keeps every grounded device (fire releasing panel, EPO station, boxes) off that wall. Full notice: `docs/audit/EN-001_working_space_liner.md`.

## 8. Least accurate (stated honestly)

- Galaxy VL frame mass (900 kg), battery cabinet footprint and the 20 kW fan-coil rating at 18 C water are design values not read from datasheets.
- ENV-LINER-NC price is an estimate; the AHJ must accept FRP over insulation over the steel shell as 'not grounded' for condition 1.
- The BEN 2000 hot aisle (900 mm) is an egress path; servicing rack-rear PDUs energised from it would need condition 2 (EN-001 §5, open).
- BEN 1 noise and runtime are model estimates until the first burn-in records exist.
